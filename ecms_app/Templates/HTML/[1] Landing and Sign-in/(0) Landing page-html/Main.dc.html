Purpose

Our Mission

To give the CCIS community one centralized, transparent, and efficient place to submit, review, and track ethical clearance applications, so that every study involving human participants is scientifically and ethically sound before data collection begins.

Direction

Our Vision

A paper-free, accountable research ethics process in which every thesis and capstone group knows where its application stands, and every reviewer can deliver timely, well-documented decisions.

About

About EthiCCIS

EthiCCIS is designed for the CCIS College Research Ethics Committee (CLREC) and the students it serves. It replaces paper forms and scattered messages with a single flow, from submission to technical evaluation, review, and certificate issuance.
